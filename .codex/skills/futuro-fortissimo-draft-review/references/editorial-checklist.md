# Futuro Fortissimo pre-publication checklist

## Voice

- Italian by default, with real accents.
- Short, operative sentences; playful where the draft already is playful.
- No corporate preamble, fake enthusiasm, recap ending, or constructed "non è X, è Y" antithesis.
- Cut about 10% when a sentence repeats its own premise.
- Keep useful English terms only when they name a concept readers actually search for.

## Copy pass

- Check apostrophes, accents, dashes, spacing, duplicate punctuation, agreement, and capitalization.
- Normalize entity names: `Hugging Face`, `OpenAI`, `Anthropic`, `EPFL`, `Moderna`, `Merck`, `Keytruda`.
- Italic or quote foreign terms consistently; do not translate a quotation silently.
- Check that every section heading matches the text beneath it.
- Re-read the sentence before and after every replacement to catch rich-text joins.

## Fact pass

- Trace every number, quotation, study phase, company claim, and causal claim to a real source.
- Prefer original incident reports, papers, trial releases, regulators, and peer-reviewed literature.
- Distinguish a paper's conclusion from an incident mentioned nearby.
- Distinguish an experimental therapy from an approved treatment.
- Distinguish company market-cap gain from a share-price multiple.
- For crop history, distinguish a commercial export cultivar from all biological varieties.
- If evidence is uncertain or current status may have changed, browse and qualify the statement.

## Metadata pass

- Visible title: recognizable FF voice and issue number.
- Subtitle: concrete nouns from the issue; no generic promise.
- Social description: one sentence, clear enough when detached from the post.
- SEO title: under 60 characters where possible.
- SEO description: 50-160 characters; include entities and status, not a keyword list.
- Slug: lowercase ASCII, hyphenated, stable, no date unless necessary.
- Tags: maximum three; choose concepts with independent search value.
- Bilingual terms: use one or two English entity phrases inside readable Italian.

## Browser safety

- Work in the saved draft, not the published post.
- Do not press Publish unless the current request explicitly includes publication.
- Use paragraph-level edits. Avoid selecting the entire rich-text body.
- After an edit beside a figure, verify the figure still exists.
- After changing linked text, verify the link target still exists.
- Wait for the saved indicator before closing or handing off the tab.

## Visual pass

- Body and metadata come first.
- Read the latest five RSS `<enclosure>` URLs with the helper script.
- Inspect each enclosure visually before drafting a cover prompt.
- Preserve recurring motifs without cloning a prior composition.
- If using the mixed style: three raw SVG/Mercatorum vignettes plus a background language derived from the current issue.
- Reject illegible typography, arbitrary collage, fake data graphics, and generic AI gloss.
