🏆 ff.117.1 Oscar-worthy days

Substack ↗

I tried to give this (and the next) newsletter a linear thread. But then I realised Newtonian linearity has been dead for a while (🍎 ff.64.2 From Newton to Einstein).

So hold on tight, because I'm taking you into the hallucinations and mental movies I witnessed in less than 24 hours. Fuelled by the “spice” of Hong Kong and Shenzhen.

Interstellar. The days felt eternal, as if near a black hole, forcing me to keep redefining concepts like:

freedom of movement: in the morning a run through the green of The Peak (Hong Kong), in the evening through the emptiness of straight, silent streets, red with flags, in Shenzhen (China). freedom of speech: listening to Cancelleria by Pinguini Tattici Nucleari while looking at Shenzhen, towered over by a huge penguin (the symbol of Tencent and WeChat, which control information in China) and a “fountain-pen skyscraper”.

The ballpoints, big bosses, tried to keep the pencils at the same social status as before, but the pencils now meant to abolish every class distinction, and at their marches in the squares they hailed the revolution […] In the free state of Cancelleria only the ballpoints remained, and they sang and celebrated their victory Pinguini Tattici Nucleari, Cancelleria

Inception. The vertigo of spaces that seem to fold in on themselves.

Black Mirror. Need I say more?

🍩 ff.117.2 Atomic doughnuts

Substack ↗

Premises:

Everything Everywhere All at Once hadn't particularly struck me. I never eat bagels. But in Shenzhen, looking for comfort, I went for a brunch as if I were in New York.

When, that same evening, back in Hong Kong, my subconscious connected these premises, these two dots, my brain nearly exploded.

In an instant, I saw my past (and future?) again: everything everywhere all at once.

The most absurd part is that I wasn't in a film, but in reality. Between Hong Kong and Shenzhen it feels like living inside a TikTok feed: here humanity is compressed into .zip format, condensed as in ChatGPT.

A reality so dense with concepts and contrasts that it becomes a black hole, where space and time lose meaning: where everything is everywhere at the same time, precisely.

🧮 ff.117.3 Simulations and algorithms

Substack ↗

Disoriented and looking for meaning - needless to say - I opened a “medium”, YouTube. The algorithm recommended this review of the film:

In the video, more unexpected connections with my past: DALL-E, which can generate anything (🎼 ff.30 DALL-E: it makes art), and Bo Burnham's film Inside, which I watched during lockdown.

And so I realised we live in a simulation, generated by algorithms that spoon-feed our brains with connections. A brain that feeds on connections (pattern recognition) to make sense of the chaos out there.

👨‍💻 ff.117.4 Breaking free from the algorithms' simulation?

Substack ↗

But how can we escape this simulation?

In the YouTube video, a possible cure for hypermodernism (the virtual one of social media and the real one of Hong Kong): pay attention to what's happening.

But then I wondered where that phrase came from.

It's from a book I'd bought before leaving. That book is from 1967 and is called - prophetically - The Medium is the Massage (let's not also connect neural stimulation, there's no time, 🎼 ff.75 Brain massages).

This extra dot made everything collapse again. Into the bagel. Into the simulation out there (or only in our brains?).

We'll come back to it.
