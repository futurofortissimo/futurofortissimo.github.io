🏯 ff.125.1 Imperial cycles

Substack ↗

Three years ago we talked about the vision of Ray Dalio, founder of Bridgewater and the 88th richest person in the world, on the possible arrival of a new world order (📈 ff.14.2 Nostradamus: Ray Dalio and the world order).

Any great power that spends more on debt service than on defense risks ceasing to be a great power. Niall Ferguson, Debt Has Always Been the Ruin of Great Powers. Is US the Next?

During this stretch of illness and recovery, I explored hidden corners of YouTube. Among the finds: recordings of the TOKEN 2049 conference in Dubai.

In one of the talks, Lily Liu (Solana Foundation; SOL = fourth cryptocurrency by market cap) showed this chart.

Even if the numbers are probably off (8000% is 80x, not 800x), the message is clear: since 1970 US GDP has grown 20 times. Public debt? 83 times.

💵 ff.125.2 Stabilising the dollar

Substack ↗

Money printing, in short, and the well-known inflation. Back then, 25 hours of work bought you one S&P 500 share; today it takes 24 days of work.

One of the answers? Stablecoins: digital currencies whose value is stabilised algorithmically.

Increasingly widespread, especially in the BRICS and Saudi Arabia, in 5 years they've overtaken VISA and Mastercard in transaction volume ($15T in 2024).

No coincidence, then, that Stripe announced the integration of Stablecoin payments.

🏮 ff.125.3 China: the next world empire?

Substack ↗

If the American empire is giving way, who will pick up the baton? In the physical world, China looks like the most credible candidate.

Having visited Shenzhen (a city built in 40 years, now the third most populous, 🏆 ff.117.1 Oscar-worthy days), I was amazed at how light years away today's China is from the one of 25 years ago, wrapped in toxic clouds of smog:

Energy consumption: from 2.5k to 10k TWh (the US stuck at 4k), 8% of it solar.

Nuclear: leader in planned plants, India second.

High-speed rail: 45,000 km starting from zero. So much for Italy's TAV… Chinese high-speed rail: 45,000 km in under 20 years (brilliantmaps x futuro fortissimo)

Stagnation, what stagnation? Storrs Hall, in his provocative book of the same name, wondered where his flying car was (😭 ff.46.3 Wasted years). The answer: right there in Shenzhen.

🌐 ff.125.4 The alternative to China: the Internet

Substack ↗

What do you do as soon as you wake up? Check your phone or admire the national flag?

With this provocation, Balaji Srinivasan (former CTO of Coinbase and general partner at A16Z, 🌐 ff.52.3 Founding your own nation) proposes an alternative to Chinese dominance: the Internet as a new nation.

The traditional State is ever more inefficient (🎼 ff.110 A State to maintain), citizens are ever more online and tired of seeing their lives swayed by the emotional/geopolitical mood swings of power-hungry leaders.

The stability of Bitcoin, the “currency of the Internet”, during the recent financial crash over Trump's tariffs, is telling.

QED. Coinbase joins the S&P 500.
