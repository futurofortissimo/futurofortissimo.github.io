🔯 ff.126.1 From extermination camps to Intel

Substack ↗

Andy Grove survived the Holocaust. Then, having emigrated to America from Hungary, he led Intel right from its birth (co-founded with Moore, the one with the law).

Despite his technical background, he reinvented himself as an operations manager, racking up so many successes that he was named Time Man of the Year 1997.

Paranormal activity. The same paranoia fuelled by a youth spent between war and Nazism saved Intel from Japanese competition: in 1985 Andy shifted the company's focus from memory (DRAM) to chips.

🐦 ff.126.2 It's not the end of Google

Substack ↗

Exterminated by ChatGPT and Perplexity? Google today is in a similar situation. 50% of its business, Google Search ($200 billion a year), is under attack. On top of that, Apple is thinking of replacing it as the default on Safari (even though Google pays $20 billion for that privilege).

At its annual Google I/O conference, the giant showed all its cards:

Veo 3, a text-to-video model with Hollywood-grade quality, consistent characters and integrated audio and dialogue. A coincidence that veo means “I see” in Spanish? Veo veo. ¿Qué ves? Una cosita. ¿Qué cosa es? Empieza por "C". ¿Qué será qué será qué será?

Simultaneous audio translation in Google Meet (keeping voice and expressiveness). AI-powered smart glasses (Android XR Glasses) and screens that can create 3D avatars (Google Beam).

❎ ff.126.3 Google's moonshot: X

Substack ↗

Here are Google's advantages over OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and Perplexity, according to its CEO Sundar Pichai:

Products everyone uses. Gmail, YouTube, Cloud. Custom chips. In-house Tensor Processing Units (TPUs), now in their 7th generation, with Ironwood, 40+ exaflops. X, Alphabet's Moonshot Factory. A forge of research on self-driving (Waymo), quantum computers and biology (🎼 ff.28 Google researches the future). Nobel. A recognition rarely given to private companies, won by Demis Hassabis for Chemistry in 2024 (🧬 ff.4.1 The structure of proteins).

In short, Google isn't just search.

🅰️ ff.126.4 AlphaEvolve

Substack ↗

The final gem. After AlphaGo and AlphaStar, Google/DeepMind recently unveiled AlphaEvolve. Not to win at video games, but to revolutionise mathematics.

While pushing the frontiers of maths (a new record of 593 spheres in the “kissing number problem” in 11 dimensions), it cuts data-centre consumption by 1% (by improving Strassen's algorithm, unbeaten since 1969).
