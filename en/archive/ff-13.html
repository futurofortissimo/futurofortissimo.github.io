📈 ff.13.1 Infinite growth?

Substack ↗

The exponential growth of the last century, in every field, is really hard to digest. In the chart below, the number of people in the world. An increase linked above all to lower infant mortality.

Lucky 7%. If you're reading this statistic, you're part of a special 7%. It's estimated that of all the human beings who have existed over the last 200,000 years, 7% are alive today.

The chart also shows in pink the annual growth rate, which peaked in the year of Woodstock. The slowdown happens when a given State and its society reach a level of prosperity, technological, cultural and educational advancement such that other factors put the brakes on population growth.

This video explains the various demographic phases well, and how improvements in living standards affect the growth rate.

🇳🇬 ff.13.2 Will Nigeria explode?

Substack ↗

One of the most populous countries in the world still in the middle of the transition is Nigeria. As you can see below, the average number of children per woman stands at 5.25, far from Italy's 1.30 or France's 1.84.

Half the children. The world average has dropped drastically over the last 50 years, going from 5 to 2.44.

Lagos, Nigeria's capital, is the fastest-growing city in the world. A YouTuber went to see what it's like.
