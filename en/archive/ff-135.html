🎭 ff.135.1 It's all a casino: manipulation

Substack ↗

We have a problem: Trump. Or rather, the possibility that the world changes with a tweet; for a war, a tariff hike, a migration ban.

Tweets from “single nodes” that create rogue waves in the hyperconnected sea around us. So much so that a new tariff threat can be worth 80 million in 30 minutes (at least for someone with access to confidential information 😉 Barron Trump).

🎲 ff.135.3 Nobel-worthy bets

Substack ↗

The distrust of “single nodes” (and of America) is all in this chart, which shows that for the first time since 1996 central banks are buying more gold than US public debt:

Accelerated by AI, the future belongs to a new superpower, between:

China, with its centralisation of data that once would have drowned any bureaucracy, but that today is handled by AI and LLMs. The Internet, with its decentralisation and free markets, exemplified by prediction markets that predict the Nobel Peace Prize 11 hours before the announcement.

💳 ff.135.4 America, Nigeria and cryptocurrencies

Substack ↗

Back to the debt of the United States of America. And to cryptocurrencies.

Let's talk numbers. In 2011, China and Japan held 23% of US debt. Today just over 6%. Who has replaced them? Probably Nigeria and Argentina.

In search of financial independence (and a currency that's stable against inflation, 👍ff.73.3 A financial VPN), developing countries are buying USDT, “digital dollars” on the blockchain.

So American debt is digitised and decentralised (ARK Invest report).

More examples of digitisation in politics:
