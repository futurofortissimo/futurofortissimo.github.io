🗺️ ff.14.1 A short deep dive on Ukraine

Substack ↗

A great map by Visual Capitalist sums up the key facts about Ukraine. In particular:

The most important cities (43 million inhabitants in total). The gas networks crossing Ukrainian territory (shrinking since 2001 anyway: from 120 to 40 bcm3). The importance of agriculture (70% of the land is farmed), so much so that Russia and Ukraine export a third of the world's wheat and a fifth of its maize. Lebanon, for example, imports 50% of its wheat from Ukraine. The Chernobyl exclusion zone, on the border with Belarus

To visualise where the population is most concentrated, there's also this map.

Low-density areas are shrunk in the cartogram. A good slice of the population is in the Donetsk area, one of the hottest zones, along with Kyiv. Controlling these areas means controlling much of the infrastructure and settlements. In short, Ukraine.

📈 ff.14.2 Nostradamus: Ray Dalio and the world order

Substack ↗

I meant to talk about this author soon. But recent events sped things up. Ray Dalio, American investor (net worth 20 billion) and founder of Bridgewater Associates, one of the most important funds in the world, recently published a book on the cycles of world order. It comes out in Italy in April.

In plain words: Ray argues that the world order reached after the Second World War is crumbling. He puts together a series of factors and indicators to work out where we are in the macro-cycle.

The macro-cycle is split into 6 phases:

Creation of a new order, with funding for research and education; Consolidation of the bureaucratic system, great industriousness of the people, technological progress; Peace and prosperity, expansion and rise of the nation's currency to world reserve status; Excess spending, lower productivity. The "dandy" phase, as I like to call it. Ever-growing inequality; Rising costs to expand and maintain global influence (see military spending), debt financing with currency devaluation; Civil or external wars, peace, creation of a new order.

Since 1500, Holland, England and now the United States have followed exactly the same trajectories.

Already with the "dollar printing" policies of the COVID period the US had entered phase 5. Now military conflict has entered the field too.

Ray, more focused on China and the Taiwan area, still draws attention to possible alliances between Russia and the communist empire. And, just these days, China wanted to send a few messages on the subject (Chinese aircraft flying over Taiwan…).
