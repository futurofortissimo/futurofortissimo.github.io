📱 ff.140.1 The era of App-fluencers

Substack ↗

A16Z is betting $20 million on Wabi.ai, a sort of YouTube/Instagram for mobile apps, to hand the internet back to individual creativity and wrest it from the monopolies of the mega-corporations. Jenny Odell (How to Do Nothing, 2020) warned: 'What's the point of building digital worlds when the physical world is crumbling?'. The third way on offer: a digital world tailored to the user, not to retention algorithms.

🛠️ ff.140.2 Vibe coding in a weekend

Substack ↗

Gemini 3 is a qualitative leap for no-code: in a single weekend you can build a complete website redesign, a nutrition app (MangiaFortissimo) and an AI-augmented reading app (LeggiFortissimo). The 'poor man's levelsio' model becomes within reach: Pieter Levels, creator of NomadList, never programmed in his life. Every model update speeds the process up — but a question arises: will the next app influencer be a human or an AI agent?

🧮 ff.140.3 Mental deflation and the academic crisis

Substack ↗

HarmonicMath's Aristotle has solved Erdős problem #124, open for 30 years: AI is reaching the summits of mathematics. Even professors like Aryeh Kontorovich (Ben-Gurion University, Israel) wonder whether it's still worth writing papers. 'Mental deflation' works like the economic kind: if tomorrow a good, or developing an idea, will cost less, there's no point investing in it today.
