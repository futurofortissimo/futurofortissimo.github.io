🌿 ff.143.1 Natural luxury

Substack ↗

When electricity and services stop being invisible, you realise that wealth is the ecosystem: clean air, visible sun, usable water. Not the city's “hipster” nature: a chemical, elemental nature, beneath the Self.

Substack ↗
