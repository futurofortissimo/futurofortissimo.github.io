🧠 ff.152.1 How Claude thinks (J-Space)

Substack ↗

Anthropic studies LLMs like a brain, dissecting the activity of their “neurons”: they've discovered the Jacobian Space. A space of words that are “thought”, while others are “said”. When we apply this technique to Claude's internal activity, we get a list of words – the contents of the J-space at that precise moment – that we can simply read. Claude processes text through a series of multiple internal stages, called layers, and by applying this technique at different layers, we can watch these silent words evolve in the J-space as the model works out what to say. What shows up in the J-space goes far beyond the text Claude is reading or writing. When Claude reads code with a bug that nobody has flagged, its J-space contains “ERROR”. When it reads the raw letters of a protein sequence, the J-space contains the protein's biological function. And so you can check Claude's thinking:
