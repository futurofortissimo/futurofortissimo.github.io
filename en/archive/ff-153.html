🪚 ff.153.1 Claude's overthinking

Substack ↗

The economy of the future will be based on squeezing LLMs to optimise their tokens (which sit in the answers, but more and more in the “thoughts” behind the answers, ☕ ff.135.4 AGI advances: the Chinese “coffee” ). On that note, GPT 5.6 Terra thinks - for the same result - 100 times less than Claude… And Kimi K3, the Chinese *open source*, agentic model, with performance comparable to Opus 5, can technically run on your home computer. Although feasible, it “thinks” very slowly (32s per token). It reminds me of: "Forty-two!" yelled Loonquawl. "Is that all you've got to show for seven and a half million years' work?" Douglas Adams, The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy PS. Apparently we were “trained” on 20 billion tokens, while Claude Opus on 300 trillion, that is 15,000 times “more reading” (minute 27.01).

🥛 ff.153.2 Fed up to the knees with milk (lactate)

Substack ↗

Remember when we talked about lactic acid, performance and cancer ( 🥇 ff.53.2 Pro athletes to the rescue )? After two years of work, Enervit has released C2:1PRO LACTATE GEL MIX. Among the collaborators is the well-known Iñigo San Millán, who also works with the UAE team. He explains “the lactate shuttle” below. PS. It's mostly useful for professionals, or for people who “burn” so much energy that they're limited only by instantaneous intake/absorption. We ordinary mortals can keep eating cakes. Or ice cream. Or subcutaneous fat.
