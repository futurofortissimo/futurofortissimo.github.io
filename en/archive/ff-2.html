🏙️ff.2.1 A model as “big” as New York

Substack ↗

Artificial intelligence makes crazy things possible. We'll see a few examples in today's futuro fortissimo. But have you seen Jim Carrey in The Shining?

All this comes at a cost, an ecological one too. "Training" artificial intelligence takes more and more energy, because of the exponential growth in the number of parameters of neuromorphic systems.

To check how the training went, there's a test: identifying objects in the ImageNet database. In 2020, the best neuromorphic system got below 10% error. To get below 5 percent error, it's estimated that training will pollute as much as New York in a month.

🇨🇳ff.2.2 Alibaba and the efficient alternative

Substack ↗

Too expensive? Made in China (no pun intended) can come to the rescue.

In text generation, OpenAI's GPT-3 is the frontier (its training cost is estimated at 12 million dollars).

This month Alibaba released an alternative model. It's called M6 and its performance is staggering: 10 trillion parameters, 50 times GPT-3 (the brain has 125 trillion synapses, to give you a sense of scale).

The incredible part? Training took 1% of GPT-3's energy consumption ("only" 512 GPUs over 10 days).

Wow. 谢谢，中国.

For an in-depth read: Alberto Romero.

🍄ff.2.5 Psychedelia, take me away
