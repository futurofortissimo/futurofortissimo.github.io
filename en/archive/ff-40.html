🦄 ff.40.1 Gotta catch ‘em all

Substack ↗

Stable Diffusion is an AI image generation technique that's showing interesting results. The output (an image generated from text) is similar to DALL-E, but the approach is different (a somewhat technical blog on it).

Starting from a generic model, you can “train” the algorithm on a subcategory of infinite styles. For example, Pokémon. So we can ask what Obama, Trump or Girl with a Pearl Earring would look like in the Kanto region.

Someone wondered what the mascot Pokémon would be for every nation in the world. Some examples:

🔮 ff.40.2 A parallel future of design

Substack ↗

Back to the future. On Instagram, An Improbable Future uses similar techniques to generate objects that mix the analogue world, futuro fortissimo and improbable design. I find them evocative: they open onto a parallel world, almost a Platonic hyperuranion.

Let's remember once again that these images are generated simply from a text description of what the artist wants to see, including the render-like style.

🍰 ff.40.4 Cakes in a thousand flavours + 1

Substack ↗

Fraternal twins. As mentioned, DALL-E and Stable Diffusion results are similar; let's look at an example of the subtle differences.

We can ask the AI to change the style or an element of the image. DALL-E did this by asking the user to “paint” over the areas to change.

A Google team proposed a less invasive editing method (it doesn't require inpainting but starts from editing just the text describing the scene, Prompt-to-Prompt Image Editing):
