🍝 ff.41.1 It's all in the filling

Substack ↗

Write the recipe for casoncelli alla bergamasca

Casoncelli alla bergamasca are a typical dish of Bergamo cuisine. Here are the ingredients and the method to prepare them:

Ingredients:

500 g 00 flour; 250 g ricotta; 100 g butter; 100 g smoked pancetta; 100 g grated parmesan; 1 egg; 1 onion; 1 celery stalk; 1 carrot; 1 garlic clove; 1/2 glass white wine; Salt and pepper to taste.

Method:

In a saucepan, sauté the finely chopped onion, celery, carrot and garlic with a little butter. Add the diced smoked pancetta and brown for a few minutes. Deglaze with the white wine and let it evaporate. In a bowl, mix the ricotta with the grated parmesan, the egg, salt and pepper. Roll the pasta out very thin and, using a pastry cutter or a glass, cut out discs. Place a little filling in the centre of each disc and fold the casoncelli into half-moons, sealing the edges well with your fingers or a fork. Cook the casoncelli in plenty of boiling salted water for 3-4 minutes, then drain and dress them with the pancetta sauce prepared earlier. Serve the casoncelli piping hot, topped with a sprinkle of grated parmesan. Enjoy your meal!

👩‍🍳 ff.41.4 Parodi who?

Substack ↗

I have in the fridge: pasta, eggs, pork loin fillet, milk, cheese, peppers, beans, apples, cream, lemon, avocado, salami. Can you suggest two recipes, a first course and a main course, starting from what I have?

With the ingredients you have in your fridge, you can make two tasty recipes, for example:

First course: tagliolini with salami and peppers. To make this recipe, start by dicing the salami and peppers, then sauté them in a pan with a little oil. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in plenty of salted water and drain it al dente. Add the pasta to the pan with the salami and peppers and add a little grated cheese. Toss the pasta and serve immediately, piping hot. Main course: lemon pork loin fillet. To make this recipe, start by marinating the pork loin fillet in a bowl with lemon juice, salt, pepper and a little oil for at least 30 minutes. Then heat a non-stick pan and cook the pork loin fillet over medium heat for about 5-6 minutes per side, until nicely browned. Meanwhile, make a lemon sauce by melting a little butter in a small pan and adding the cream and lemon juice. Pour the sauce over the meat and serve hot, with a side of vegetables.
