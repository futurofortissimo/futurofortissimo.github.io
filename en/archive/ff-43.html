⛷️ ff.43.1 Is skiing disappearing with the snow?

Substack ↗

I grew up surrounded by skiers in Northern Italy. For me, winter and Christmas mean races, skis on my feet and the smell of ski wax.

A recent newsletter by Adam Tooze touched me deeply: it's about snow and skiing. Even though my father runs a Ski Club, I'd never thought about quantifying this world, which for me is synonymous with a distant, hazy youth.

But that's what we do on 🎼ff, right? We put numbers on everything. So let's go!

Here are visitors by region (millions):🇪🇺 195 |🇨🇳 17 |🇺🇸 70 |🇯🇵 30 |

Breakdown by country (with % of population) |🇩🇪14.6 M (18%) |🇮🇹 4.9 M (8%) |🇳🇴1.1 M (13%) |

57 million ski weeks every year worldwide. Despite economic growth, globally the annual number of ski days is 400 M. The Alps account for 52% of global ski days (210 M). In the United States the figure has held steady between 50 and 59 million a year for the last 20 years:

But where do we go? Austria attracts loads of foreigners, while Italy has half the visits of France and Austria (and fewer than Japan, too!). Through my “local” lens, I'd have said the bulk of the European business was between the Dolomites, Lombardy and Valle d'Aosta.

🎁 ff.43.3 Last-minute artsy gift ideas

Substack ↗

Now let's look at some last-minute art and design gifts.

David Hockney recently came out with My Window (published by Taschen). It collects 120 drawings made on iPhone and iPad, along with observations from the window of his home in Yorkshire.

Candle voodoo. Have you ever looked at your partner, your parents or your pet and thought, "Wow, you'd make perfect candles"? Janie Korn creates personalised candles of your loved ones. Forget mistletoe, nothing is more Christmassy than setting your boyfriend's head on fire.

Bingo! Wild Cards by Canopy and Stars wants to help us reconnect with nature one card game at a time. Designed together with a human ecologist, these cards are the perfect solution for anyone wanting to rediscover tribal harmony. Just don't kill each other over the tombola.
