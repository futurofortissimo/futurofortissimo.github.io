🎨 ff.45.2 Art and charts from 2022

Substack ↗

If 2021 was all metaverse, NFTs or some crypto project, 2022 was inflation, Fed interest rates and crypto winter. The stock market didn't do well (except for the energy sector).

ItsNiceThat also collected the most beautiful art projects of 2022. Feast your eyes (it's not just Indian Teletubbies)!

FlowingData, meanwhile, collected the most eye-catching data visualisations. Neal.fun mixes sociological research with a touch of irony. For example, the Absurd Trolley Problem asks whether you'd rather sacrifice yourself or 5 sentient robots (and lots of other fun stuff).

Elsewhere you can also review earthly things like hiccups or beards.
