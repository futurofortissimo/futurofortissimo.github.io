🗣️ ff.48.1 Everyone is talking about ChatGPT

Substack ↗

ChatGPT : 2023 = NFTs : 2022. Too bad it all came crashing down afterwards. Unlike “non-fungible” digital assets, what's happened is staggering:

1 million users in 5 days |OpenAI is 7 years old |ChatGPT = 15 of OpenAI's $29B (Ferrari = $49B)

Iron Maiden. 5 days means that, out of nowhere, the OpenAI folks invented a digital concert attended by 200 thousand different people every day. For a whole working week. Then a weekend hangover.

Less friction, more use. It's incredible, because OpenAI playground (same technology) was already accessible, but nobody played with it. At OpenAI they changed the web interface and the company exploded (OK, maybe underneath there's also the text-to-model-training angle).

🐰 ff.48.2 Steve Jobs and the Playboy interview

Substack ↗

In the chart above there was obviously the iPhone too. And that brings us to Apple. Let me explain: with prompting, you can convince ChatGPT to impersonate Thales, Leonardo da Vinci or Darwin (or anyone else who has put their thinking out there).

In a Playboy interview in 1985, Steve Jobs foresaw this future:

Immagina di poter catturare la visione del mondo di Aristotele e i principi fondamentali della sua visione del mondo. Potresti effettivamente porre una domanda ad Aristotele! OK. Non sarebbe esattamente Aristotele, forse sarebbe una pessima imitazione, o forse no. Steve Jobs, Intervista per Playboy, Feb 1985

After all, who can say what Aristotle would answer to our questions?

Stelfie the Time Traveller is all the rage on social media. He takes selfies across historical eras. Among other things, he even met Tesla (remember: these images aren't made with Photoshop, they're AI renderings, asked for the improbable historical photo).

we'll have conversations with bots holding the worldview of whoever we want, we'll animate them in 3D. Ready for a psychoanalysis/torture session/passion with Freud/Jesus?

🤹 ff.48.3 AI, jack of all trades

Substack ↗

A roundup of more serious applications.

McKinsey (because it's not a 🎼ff without them) gathers in one chart all the applications GPT can have in business. The leap we've made with this technology is comparable to the one we got thanks to Google search. The information was there, but it was hard to access.

Move over, Cambridge Analytica. Kantrowitz asked the bot to imagine its own chimera. Quoting directly the dystopian future imagined by the AI:

And just like in the story of Frankenstein, the true horror of ChatGPT isn’t just in its appearance, but in its actions. Imagine a world where chatbots like ChatGPT are able to spread misinformation and manipulate people on a massive scale, without anyone being able to tell that they are not human

Relax. Like a good lobbyist, the bot will convince you there's nothing to worry about. Let me explain. They showed that GPT-3 (used by ChatGPT) can estimate better than its predecessor (da-vinci-002) whether a law is of interest to a company. If it is, it writes a persuasion letter to steer policy choices.

Trust me. Oh, and then someone asked it to explain HTML code to a dog.

⚠️ ff.48.4 ChatGPT's (mathematical) limits

Substack ↗

Numbers lie. Wolfram Alpha, a little scared, snitched to the teacher that ChatGPT tried to copy. Jokes aside, when it comes to maths, the bot isn't top of the class (after all, it “studied” for another subject: finding the most “truthful” way to continue a sentence)

And it gets it wrong, by a lot.

ChatGPT is a bit like that know-it-all classmate who never applied himself but still brought home his B, with storytelling and rhetorical intrigues worthy of Arist…, er, Cicero.

Let's close with a super-important message for all of us. Don't use ChatGPT to calculate integrals.
