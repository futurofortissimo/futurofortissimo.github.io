🥃 ff.54.1 The context: the bitter taste of powerlessness

Substack ↗

Yes, yet another newsletter about GPT. Sorry, but there's way too much going on to just move on.

Exponential improvements. Not least because the recent GPT-4 is genuinely superior to the previous version (150 pages from Microsoft Research) (ChatGPT used v3.5). As a preview, here are a few examples of the quality of its output.

Context is everything, as LetMeTellIt suggests in What will become of content? Here's the backstory: after a first, promising date with a girl (and with a second date already confirmed) I receive this tear-jerking message.

It's true, we hadn't talked about it, and in your shoes I'd probably have raised the issue the same way. But actually, turning down the second date wasn't driven by a fear that we might want different things. In other words: I didn't think you'd shot off like a spring towards something serious. I pulled back because I'm not at peace emotionally, and that's affecting parts of my everyday emotional life it shouldn't affect. Translating that into other words too: I need to calm the storm, but on my own. Basically you couldn't care less, but your request to try going out was more than reasonable and deserved a fuller answer than "it's not the right time". Anyway, it turned into a rambling essay [sic!]. In the end you get stuck with the bitter taste of powerlessness, it sucks, I know, I'm aware of it. Thank you for trying to get something nice and pleasant out of it anyway.

Prompt: rewrite the message using only words that start with A.

Author admits axing appointment. Acute affective anxiety. Admits acrimony and appreciates another's attitude.

There. GPT-3, for example, couldn't do this.

📜 ff.54.2 A Petrarch sonnet

Prompt: rewrite the message as if it were a Petrarch sonnet.

Ma il rifiutar un'altra poste non fiaDa timor che voler divergente osa,Né ch'al serio legar mi credessi arcano.

Retraggo fuor di me, non son queta,Perché l'emotion che in me sconvolgeSul quotidiano aspett tocco non unda.

Bisogno ancor ho di placar la calmaStormire in solitudo soffronda. No'l curi, te ne cale, fia giust'esempio,Chiedendo delle volte avanzare,Benché "non vien l'istante" esser risposta.

Vanteria è al fin, sapor amaro invito,L’impotenza, trista, il broncio scernoGrazie rendo cercando, è vero, e porgendo,Di gioie assaporar nel bello e diletto

The “non vien l'istante” (“the moment comes not”) broke my heart.

⚽️ ff.54.3 Caressa's play-by-play commentary

Substack ↗

Among friends we love recounting love affairs against a football and transfer-market backdrop.

Prompt: Rewrite the message as football commentary

And here we are, friends, in a new match of this fascinating love story. Our protagonist, having intercepted her opponent's pass about a possible second date, decides to play on the counter and close down the lanes so he can't break through to her heart.

In midfield, in a tête-à-tête with an open and sincere attitude, they discuss why she turned down the invitation to spend more time together. She acknowledges that her opponent's move was very clever in trying to penetrate [sic!] her defences, but admits she first has to sort out some personal issues to be at peace on the pitch.

The message lands like a pinpoint cross into the box: our champion needs time to calm the storm inside her before she can consider playing alongside others in her squad.

def second_date(rejection_reason):

if rejection_reason == "fear of wanting different things":

return False

else:

return True

def emotional_state(state):

if state == "not at peace":

return True

else:

return False

def try_going_out(request):

if request == "reasonable":

return "deserves a fuller answer than 'it's not the right time'"

else:

return "not reasonable"

rejection_reason = "not fear of wanting different things"

emotional_state = "not at peace"

request = "reasonable"

if second_date(rejection_reason) and emotional_state(emotional_state):

print("I need to calm the storm, but on my own.")
