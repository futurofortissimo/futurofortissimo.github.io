🐸 ff.59.1 Stagnation? Hardly!

Substack ↗

Born in France in the mid-1800s, positivism is the cultural glue of technology. Dismissed as a short-sighted pursuit of the useful, lacking ethical/psychological analysis, it is undoubtedly the cultural foundation of the technological society we live in.

Back to 1971 and the supposed technological stagnation (🎼 ff.50 WTF happened in 1971?)? According to Works in Progress, it's a big fat lie:

GDP doesn't capture progress. With technology we achieve more while spending less, in a deflationary drop in consumption (energy included). Digital for free. There's a famous study showing that the value of digital services can reach €25,000 a year. That's money we don't spend (and that doesn't land in our pay packets) but that we should take into account. Online, we don't spend. The data on spending on online services got me thinking. Even though we now spend a quarter of our day in front of a screen, we're particularly stingy online.

By the way, if you'd like to buy me a digital coffee (I drink a lot of them) you can click here. Or simply share this newsletter. Share

Once writing is invented, you have to learn to read! Increasing computing power has been the main focus of the last 30 years. Let's say we focused on optimising the ink and the writing. Now, thanks to training and boomers being replaced, we can start writing books! It's time, as the Progressive Policy Institute argues, to “smash” the Great Stagnation.

🗃️ ff.59.2 Social pressure and bureaucracy

Substack ↗

You can study technology with an evolutionary theory: just as a species evolves based on external pressures (or their absence), technological development goes hand in hand with society's acceptance/resistance.

Technological co-evolution. QWERTY is far from the optimal keyboard layout, but we adapted and now changing it is a mess. Another example of society-technology interaction is the nuclear freeze after Fukushima and the rethink after the invasion of Ukraine.

If you want to dig deeper, I suggest the book Come saremo by Telmo Pievani and Luca de Biase.

➕ ff.59.3 Why telling positive stories matters

Substack ↗

In the newsletter Not Boring, Packy McCormick points out that, despite the fact that:

AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) is around the corner; we're sending ever bigger rockets into orbit; we're reprogramming T cells to fight tumours (!!!)

all around us there's nothing but fear and negativity:

La ricerca spaziale è vista come un passatempo per miliardari. Le centrali nucleari stanno chiudendo in tutto il mondo. I progressi nell’AI suscitano timori di perdita di lavoro e di essere trasformati in clip di carta. I progressi nella biologia sintetica fanno pensare a focolai incontrollabili e alla minaccia del bioterrorismo. È difficile biasimare le persone. Lo show più discusso in TV al momento -The Last of Us- si svolge in un mondo in cui, secondo NPR: “il cambiamento climatico ha alimentato la diffusione di un nuovo patogeno, che si diffonde in tutto il mondo infettando gli esseri umani, trasformandoli in zombie e controllando il loro cervello

Elliot Hershberg@ElliotHershbergThe fact that we can hot-wire a patients own T cells to attack certain types of tumors is absolutely incredible.

And the thing is, we're just scratching the surface when it comes to cell therapy. 8:00 PM ∙ Feb 7, 2023172Likes19Retweets

The ever brilliant Balaji Srinivasan, at the height of the pandemic, published The Purpose of Technology, a hymn to positivity, calling for creative, progressive scientists who care about outreach. Real stars.

Scienziati e ingegneri, progressisti piuttosto che conservatori tecnologici, devono imparare a scrivere, pubblicare e dirigere. Dobbiamo costruire consapevolmente un ecosistema mediatico decentralizzato guidato dalla tecnologia. Non tweet, ma articoli. Non articoli, ma video. Non video, ma lungometraggi. E non film, ma un’intera libreria di contenuti originali Netflix, un ecosistema mediatico tecnologico parallelo pieno di contenuti ispirazionali per i progressisti tecnologici. Una vita intera di contenuti che sostengono la moneta immutabile, la frontiera infinita, l’intelligenza artificiale e la vita eterna. Balaji Srinivasan,The purpose of Technology

Chaudhri, the heart of the design and software behind the invention of the iPhone, shares this idea too and lists his heroes and sources of inspiration. Young people need a Maradona of technology to cheer for. Of positivity. Neil deGrasse Tyson isn't enough.

I want to find meaning. Here's futuro fortissimo's: to stimulate, to spread knowledge, to prepare society for the fortissimo future. In short, future-forming.
