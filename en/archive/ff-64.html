🔦 ff.64.1 The exhibition on the void

Substack ↗

Returnable void. For millennia philosophy has pondered “being” and “non-being”. Matter and void. More recently, matter and antimatter, even going so far - with virtual particles - as to deny that a void is possible.

In Bergamo, Capital of Culture 2023 together with Brescia, the exhibition Salto nel vuoto (Leap into the void). Its common thread: the dematerialisation/digitalisation of art and, therefore, of life. Of being.

The retrospective has plenty of nods to the metaverse, NFTs and generative art. And so a philosophising 🎼ff is born, on the present and future of our society.

Speaking of humanism, I recently discovered Trasumanare by Emilio Dal Bo: for lovers of literature, personal growth and philosophy.

🍎 ff.64.2 From Newton to Einstein

Substack ↗

Information (created ever faster with AI) is replacing the object. With the blockchain, the digital gives value to itself.

The only limit? The speed of computation and of light. In a beautiful parallel with the history of physics, Byung-Chul Han wonders whether, between the metaverse and social media, we're moving from Newton's world of “billiard balls” to Einstein's relativistic one. In this process, just as special relativity predicts, we end up “squashed”.

PS: Han also wrote The Burnout Society (see 🎼 ff.62 How to avoid burnout).

🔗 ff.64.3 Just links connecting information?

Substack ↗

In a 1989 book, Angels Fear, Catherine and Gregory Bateson tell the story of a mother, elderly and perhaps suffering from Alzheimer's, who chimes in on discussions between her son and his group of friends, dubbed “cyberneticians”:

Voi che parlate di reperimento delle informazioni, ma che ne sapere di queste cose? Io si che me ne intendo, perché ho perso la memoria. L’unico sistema che ho per trovare le cose è di tenere un pochino di ogni cosa dappertutto.

Backup. It seems the ever more forgetful modern human is just like that: leaving a bit of information in every corner, without even having to remember where, whether in a Notion database (accessible from phone and PC, of course) or in cold newsletters full of URLs.

👨‍💻 ff.64.4 Matrix and matter

Substack ↗

Cloud, Internet of Things, 5G, QR codes, augmented reality blended into the “real” one. Screens, Oculus or Apple's Vision Pro are our portals. Related: Non-things, how we stopped living the real.

Matter has been replaced by the Matrix

Urbanisation took away our greenery and animals, digitalisation our concrete and asphalt. micmer

Around us, ever more digital mental maps

La società ha sostituito a tal punto la realtà delle cose con simboli e segni che l’esperienza umana stessa è divenuta una simulazione del realeBudrillard,Simulacri e simulazioni(1981)

For example:

a tree → source of fruit → firewood → subject for an Instagram photo

Futurism, a coincidence? Matter (materia) and mother (madre) are etymologically linked. Perhaps it's no accident that Boccioni, pioneer of Futurism and of dematerialising figures, chose his mother on the terrace for the work titled (needless to say) Materia.

We can give a curious (and anachronistic) new reading to Foscolo in this sense too

Tu non altro che il canto avrai del figlio,O materna mia terra; a noi prescrisseIl fato illacrimata sepoltura. Ugo Foscolo, A Zacinto

OK. I can now wrap up this rambling collection of thoughts. I'm dematerialising.
