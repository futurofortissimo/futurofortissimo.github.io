🎵ff.7.5 Spotify Wrapped

Substack ↗

Below are also the most-streamed songs globally, on Spotify and Apple Music. I only know one of them and I feel a bit of a boomer.

Music is a very intimate act, often tied to our moods or to social belonging. Our choices of podcasts, music and playlists, together with location and targeting, can say a lot about who we are and which emotional state best represents us at that moment.

That's why Vox "unwrapped" Spotify Wrapped, the annual showcase of what Spotify's algorithms do to us, which we promptly have to make public:

Tuttavia, Spotify Wrapped diventa virale. La nostra passione collettiva per questo riassunto rivelafino a che punto gli algoritmi si sono integrati nel modo in cui concepiamo noi stessi nella cultura del consumo digitale: come marchi da perfezionare. Kelly Pau,Spotify Wrapped, unwrapped(Vox)

For example, despite the strong push towards gender equality/neutrality, to sign up for Spotify you still have to state your sex, which is then used as a control parameter for the algorithms (song skip frequency varies with gender).

🐆ff.7.7 Anything you need, sir?

Substack ↗

Ok, with the previous calendar filled, we need a new one. So how could we not bring in Leopardi (see the emoji above).

What do you want from this 2022? Besides complete freedom from the pandemic, of course. Do you want it to be like the one that just ended, the one before, or simply left to chance, like all the ones lived so far, after all?

Passeggere: “Oh che vita vorreste voi dunque?”Venditore: “Vorrei una vita così, come Dio me la mandasse, senz’altri patti.”Passeggere: “Una vita a caso, e non saperne altro avanti, come non si sa dell’anno nuovo?”Venditore: “Appunto.”Giacomo Leopardi,Operette morali
