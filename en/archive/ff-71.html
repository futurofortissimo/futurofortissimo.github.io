🤖ff.71.2 ChatGPT sees (you)…

Substack ↗

Imagination, life is your creationAqua,Barbie Girl

It's not 🎼ff without ChatGPT. For a year now this wacky acronym (which stands for Chat Generative Pre-trained Transformer) has been filling our screens. As of this week, we can quiz GPT with an image.

Cognition and sight are the founding pillars of our evolutionary success. Now artificial intelligence combines them, with results even better than the average human's. Fortissimo or terrifyingissimo?

Where's Waldo? Someone asked it to find Waldo.

Or to interpret a text written in illegible handwriting (pharmacists all over the world say thank you).

But that's not all. Hold on tight.

📸ff.71.3 And snaps your picture!

Substack ↗

hellokillian developed open-interpreter. Basically, a ChatGPT that runs scripts to control your PC (yes, this can have very unwanted effects, which is why it asks for confirmation before running each command).

You can ask it in plain words to edit a video, add subtitles or switch your operating system to dark theme. In words, without opening software (and getting lost in its graphical interface) or touching the mouse.

Selfie-AI stick. MikeBirdTech showed how to use it on his phone. Without touching it, by voice control, it checks the battery status and takes a photo.
