Yes, I'm a bit obsessed with cryptocurrencies. And in this 🎼ff I'll explain why.

But, but, but. The A16Z fund has introduced an index to track the health of the sector (State of Crypto Index, which combines the number of active users, projects and trading liquidity across the various blockchains).

🤖ff.73.2 The AI–Bitcoin convergence

Substack ↗

The charts seem to show stagnation. What's changed recently? AI, ChatGPT and MidJourney. As discussed here:

A few takeaways:

The economy of converting money into compute time is exploding (along with the energy needed, so much so that Microsoft is considering nuclear reactors). VISA/Mastercard take up to a month to process payments for these services, but companies like OpenAI offer instant access to compute. The compute-time/hardware mismatch (hardware that can also be used to mine BTC) becomes a dangerous inefficiency in the system. What's more, VISA/Mastercard aren't accessible to everyone. Even verified users with accounts on these networks have seen their access blocked (privacy watchdog, anyone?). In these cases, the blockchain was the only way in.

👍ff.73.3 A financial VPN

Substack ↗

In short, crypto guarantees access to ever more essential digital services, like a financial VPN.

No wonder Worldcoin (👁️ ff.38.3 The cryptocurrency to reduce inequality, backed by OpenAI founder Sam Altman) has reached 2.2M users.

Another reason crypto is the future is demographics. ChainAnalysis's map shows global adoption is high in India and Nigeria (2 billion people in 2050).

Not just individuals. Entire nations crushed by inflation could get financial revenge (Javier Milei, a candidate in Argentina, is leaning precisely on these themes).

❌ff.73.5 X: Elon's revenge?

Substack ↗

What do Twitter and Elon Musk have to do with all this?

The X rebrand signals the platform's transformation into a “Western WeChat” able to integrate YouTube, WhatsApp and, above all, PayPal.

A big circle closing: in 1999 Elon founded X.com itself, a proto digital bank later absorbed into PayPal. Now with X he wants to build payments into a social network rising from the ashes of Twitter.

XXX. Musk really is obsessed. He recently unveiled x.AI, and the unknown X hides in the logos of SpaceX, Neuralink and in his son's name, XÆA-12.
