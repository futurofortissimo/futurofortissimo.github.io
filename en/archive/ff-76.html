🫁ff.76.3 And the physical effects

Substack ↗

Yes, some trends have passed. But the psycho-physical mark COVID left is very much current.

65 million. The number of people with long COVID. The scars left behind: a mix of shortness of breath, poor concentration, insomnia and bad sleep quality.

Long-Covid Europe lets you play Minecraft with a character weakened by COVID. It's a provocation between the real and the digital, reminding us we can't escape what happened through Zoom and VR.

Oh, related: Nature discusses how the microbiome of babies born during lockdown was altered (and remember how much we are our microbiome, so much so that Zoe links it to the glycaemic response, 🕛 ff.35.2 Smartwatches and more).

🐛 ff.76.4 Turned into a cockroach?

Substack ↗

Bruno Latour (whom we bumped into in 🧼 ff.69.2 Quantum foam) proposes a philosophy of lockdown.

Ska-ska cockroaches. According to the philosopher, lockdown turned us into a cockroach, as happened to Gregor Samsa in Kafka's Metamorphosis. Not because it physically confined us within four walls, but because it mentally confirmed our materiality.

Expanding on Down to Earth: Politics in the New Climatic Regime, Latour argues that lockdown brought us down to Earth, reversing the push towards inert space that began with Galileo, continued with Ariosto and culminated with NASA and Musk.

Stiamo iniziando a renderci conto che non abbiamo, che non avremo mai, che nessuno ha mai avuto l’esperienza di incontrare “cose inerti”. Bruno Latour,After Lockdown

A SpaceX-style landing? The impossibility of living “inertly”, “above the Earth” is the lesson of the pandemic. COVID, with its mucus, plunged us into Gaia's mud. Into Gregor's exoskeleton.

Non è giusto spingerci a tornare sulla Terra, se non ci dicono dove atterrare per non schiantarci. Bruno Latour,After Lockdown
