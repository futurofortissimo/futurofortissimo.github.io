🎄ff.79.4 The Christmas tree effect

Substack ↗

Unwrapping the year. Thanks in part to Spotify Wrapped, Christmas and the end of the year come with a renewed bout of introspection. More and more, a moment to stop and take stock.

But what can Christmas teach us?

More is worse. American journalist David Epstein, 40, compares our brain to an over-decorated Christmas tree (The Christmas Tree Effect):

In Texas, road safety awareness signs increased mental load, causing more accidents. The US Army had to cut down the gear issued to soldiers, which was weighing them down too much.

Over-decorating doesn't seem healthy.

Labor limae. Faced with a problem, we look for a new product, a new diet, a new subscription/service. Leidy Klotz, in Subtract, suggests subtracting, removing, simplifying.

This Christmas we could write a special letter: asking for something to be taken away. P.S. To go with Trading Places, I suggest Netflix's documentary Minimalism.

before you switch tabs and put on Michael Bublé, remember to leave a little heart below, and merry christmas
