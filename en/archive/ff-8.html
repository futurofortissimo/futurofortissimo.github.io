📺 ff.8.1 What shall we watch tonight?

Substack ↗

The main form of entertainment is - historically - TV. I found an analysis of the IMDb catalogue showing the types of content offered over the last 70 years.

In particular, you can spot the various peaks for:

Music in the post-war years; Drama in the 50s followed by romantic drama in the 80s; Westerns in the 60s and reality shows in the 2000s; Talk shows and news, which seem to have the upper hand today (let alone with COVID).

What genre would you put Squid Game in? With its 111 million views, it will cause quite a spike in the chart above.

📱 ff.8.3 Small screens

Substack ↗

(Smart) TV may well be the most traditional and comfortable way to enjoy content, which increasingly comes from online platforms. But, but, but…

Size doesn't matter: smartphones generate far more revenue than TV and PC sales. And smartphone accessories far outsell other tech products, like tablets, wearable electronics, consoles and more.

The multiverse is, for now, confined to a tiny screen.

💰 ff.8.4 Advertising revenue

Substack ↗

Testifying to the trend towards the smartphone, mobile advertising revenue has recently overtaken TV's, going from 20.8% to 34% share in less than a lustrum. I hadn't used that word in a while (at least five years).

🎮 ff.8.5 Killing boredom (and not human beings)

Substack ↗

Where do video games fit into all this?

Benedict Evans@benedictevansPlaystation has about 5bn hours of play a month, which happens to be almost exactly the same as the top 10 series and movies on Netflix in October7:17 PM ∙ Nov 18, 2021405Likes57Retweets

One hour, per person, per month. The time spent playing PlayStation by all PSN users equals (5 billion hours) the viewing time of the 10 most popular Netflix series.

But if you consider that Netflix's catalogue is much bigger, it seems TV series eclipse video games, at least in total hours. The average time per user could be much higher for consoles (I know from personal experience).

Video games can reduce violence. An article tracked the frequency of crimes and violent acts following the release of Grand Theft Auto V. In the first two months after release, a significant drop was observed!

⌛ ff.8.7 Killing time

Substack ↗

To wrap up this digression on boredom, a thought on the need (or not) to kill time. Reading is also a weapon we have to do it, right?

Da quanto tempo sei vivo? Prendi gli anni, moltiplicali per 365 e poi per 24. Quante ore hai vissuto? Cosa devi mostrare per tutti loro? La risposta per molte persone è: non abbastanza. Abbiamo così tante ore che le diamo per scontate. Quello che possiamo mostrare per il nostro tempo su questo pianeta sono partite di golf, anni trascorsi in ufficio, tempo passato a guardarefilm mediocri, una pila di libri stupidi che a malapena ricordiamo di aver letto eforse un garage pieno di giocattoli. Siamo come il personaggio diThe Long Goodbyedi Raymond Chandler: "Soprattutto, ammazzo il tempo", dice, "ed è duro a morire". Ryan Holiday,The Daily Stoic

Well, I hope I didn't bore you.
