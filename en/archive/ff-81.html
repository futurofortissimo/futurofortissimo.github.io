➕ff.81.1 Techno-optimism

Substack ↗

Lux Capital is already writing 2024 into the history books. Let's see why.

2023 closed with renewed optimism about the future, symbolised by Marc Andreessen's The Techno-Optimist Manifesto.

Over the top and idealistic. As every manifesto should be. Here are the lines that struck me most.

Crediamo che l’Intelligenza Artificiale sia la nostra alchimia, la nostra pietra filosofale. Stiamo letteralmente facendo pensare la roccia, la sabbia, il silicio.

Crediamo in una spirale crescente. L’energia come motore della civiltà. Più energia abbiamo, più persone possiamo sostenere. Crediamo sia possibile generare energia in modo gratuito, tanto da arrivare a darla per scontata come una penna, nonostante una penna sia tutt’altro che un oggetto semplice (così come è internet). Dobbiamo rendere scontato ogni oggetto fisico.

Crediamo che la popolazione mondiale possa raggiungere i 50 miliardi di persone e diffondersi su altri pianeti.

The incredible growth of world wheat production.

Il nostro nemico è la stagnazione. Il nostro nemico è l’anti. Anti-meritocrazia. Anti-ambizione. Anti-conquista. Anti-grandezza. Il nostro nemico è la burocrazia, la veto-crazia, la deferenza cieca delle tradizioni.

The Christian Creed? To wrap up, a list of “secular saints” (it made me smile):

[…] Andy Warhol, Bertrand Russell, Jeff Bezos, Filippo Tommaso Marinetti, John Galt, Paul Collier […]

Ah, I'd barely finished writing the newsletter when George Hotz (geohot, the nerd behind the first iPhone jailbreak) weighed in too.

🌡️ff.81.3 COP28's good resolutions

Substack ↗

COP28 gathered end-of-year/New Year's resolutions to “slim down emissions”:

22 nations (UK, France, US) want to triple nuclear power by 2050 (Italy is thinking about it). 3x sustainable energy production by 2030 too (up to 11 TW, = 96k TWh a year; in 2022 fossil sources reached 137k TWh). Nerd corner. To convert energy production (11 TW) into annual consumption (TWh), multiply production by 8,760 (hours in a year). Electrifying Europe with Clean Tech: 6x heat pumps and 15x electric vehicle production by 2050. At COP28, $100B of investment was pledged (Alterra $30B UAE and Adani Green Energy $22B India). The transition needs 20x that money every year (🎼 ff.16 Money, money: the ecological transition).

🩹ff.81.4 Gene therapies

Substack ↗

2024 also kicks off with a bang thanks to the FDA approval of the first CRISPR/Cas9-based gene therapy, which fights sickle cell anaemia (20M people worldwide).

Another example of acceleration. In less than 10 years we've gone from discovering a technology (Nobel Prize 2020) to using it for therapy (🧬 ff.66.4 ARK, RNA, AI and other acronyms).

🦾ff.81.5 Augmented by AI

Substack ↗

In the history books for a new human-computer interface, too?

A new touchscreen. Years after the iPhone revolution, Apple is now getting serious about augmented reality with its Vision Pro.

GPT sees and speaks. While Gemini's “salesy” demo raised eyebrows, it's clear that ChatGPT-style language models (LLMs) are increasingly multimodal: they accept visual and voice input.

Meanwhile, scientists and PhDs all over the world are reaching new, faster scientific discoveries.

The future is ever more fortissimo. Happy 2024!
