🥽ff.85.1 A new “iPhone moment”?

Substack ↗

In the last futuro fortissimo we talked about the screens shown at CES (🎼 ff.84 CES: the tech craft fair). And how, in my view, they were already outdated.

With the launch of Vision Pro, Apple reshuffles the deck on how we access digital reality. And when Apple sets out to do something, it means business. OK, except for cars (Project Titan since 2014).

With simple gestures and no Wii-style joysticks, Vision Pro moves the technology from “interesting, but for nerds” (Meta's Oculus) to “people lose their minds seeing the headset around New York”.

Fahrenheit 451 talks about wall-sized TVs. In Himels Tech's YouTube video, a house tour with screens everywhere. With Gordon Ramsay dictating recipes, floating above the pan.

More early Vision Pro applications, via Morning Brew.

✳️ff.85.2 Generative convergence

Substack ↗

Back to the future. Integrating 3D objects is getting easier and easier thanks to AI. For example, Ammaar used a text-to-3D-object AI to recreate the DeLorean and parked it in his living room.

Dematerialisation. Just as maps, cameras and alarm clocks were swept away by the iPhone, will furniture, windows and plants meet the same fate?

This blend of generative AI and VR may explain MidJourney's recent hire (the leader in quality of AI-generated images) of the engineer behind Vision Pro.

Mouth-watering generation. Creating 3D objects is so easy (for example with Polycam) that below you'll find the baked pasta I ate at a friend's place last weekend.

🍝ff.85.4 Carbonara and cryptocurrencies

Substack ↗

Finally, it's not futuro fortissimo without a little crypto sprinkled like grana over your carbonara.

Endless guanciale. Since we'll be ever more sick of digital content (♾️ ff.72.1 Digital infinity), to “digest” this “generative guanciale” (texts, images and objects) we need to validate their history, their “singularity in digital space-time”.

Launched in 2023, the Bitcoin Inscription lets you tie every satoshi, a “Bitcoin cent/copper”, to digital data, uniquely and unambiguously.
