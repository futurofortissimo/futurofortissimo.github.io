💀 ff.86.1 If you don't move, you die

Substack ↗

If you move, you lower your probability of dying from any cause over the next ten years.

As the chart shows, all-cause mortality drops by up to 50% as metabolic fitness (MET) rises.

MET what? Metabolic equivalents are the unit that measures our body's metabolic consumption.

1 MET = 3.5 ml/kg/min O2 (the O2 consumed just to stay alive)

Below, oxygen consumption by type of exercise (dashes) and the population distribution of VO2max, which drops as we age (lines).

Running at 16 km/h equals 16 METs, for example. Walking, 3 METs.

To not die (or to die with lower probability) you need to rack up at least 15-30 MET-hours of activity a week.

🟠ff.86.3 Strava's 2023 report

Substack ↗

Speaking of Strava. Have you had a look at the annual report? Here are some highlights from Year in Sport - The Trend Report 2023 Strava (via Luca Merelli):

The most popular activities of the year: running, cycling and walking (followed by their off-road versions, trail running and gravel/MTB).

Youth anxiety (🏃🏻 ff.68.1 Ever faster) shows up in shorter, faster activities for Gen Z.

Watching their knees, Boomers prefer the bike (2x the activities compared to running). Young people cycle to work: among Gen Z, commuters doubled in the last year.

Why do sport? Diet, goals/races and good music (rock, though hip hop too). But also a trip to the bathroom (🎼 ff.17 Sport (and the people who poop it)).
