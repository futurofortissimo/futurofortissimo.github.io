1️⃣ff.89.1 A podcast worth a listen

Substack ↗

We're 3 months into 2024. How are those New Year's resolutions going? Goals reached?

Asked about his resolutions for 2024, Greg McKeown, Stanford MBA and Mormon bishop (not your average guy, in short), told Tim: not goals, people.

Greg keeps a journal, strictly on paper, with the names of the people closest to him. He builds his actions, his goals, starting from them.

What a twist! He fascinatingly flips the world of resolutions, goals and to-do lists of our hyper-individualistic society on its head.

🔺ff.89.2 Maslow's pyramid 2.0

Substack ↗

In 🔺 ff.62.4 The pyramid inside all of us we saw that, as food and economic security grow, people reach for higher values to be happy. One above all: self-actualisation.

But, but, but. Greg points out, catching even the expert Ferriss off guard, that in the last phase of his life Maslow revisited the Pyramid, adding self-transcendence.

🤴ff.89.3 What is essential is invisible to the eye

Substack ↗

How do you transcend the self?

By stopping the flood of endless options, side hustles and relationships the digital world serves up, as fast as swipes on Tinder (♾️ ff.44.2 The infinitude out there).

In short, by becoming essentialists.

I grandi amano le cifre. Quando parlate di un nuovo amico, non interessano alle cose essenziali [sic!].Non si domandano mai: “Qual è il tono della sua voce? Quali sono i suoi giochi preferiti? Fa collezione di farfalle?”Ma vi domandano: “Che età ha? Quanti fratelli? Quanto pesa? Quanto guadagna suo padre?”.Allora soltanto credono di conoscerlo.Antoine de Saint-Exupéry,Il piccolo principe.

And since 1943, that's all there is to say.

🧭ff.89.4 Constant readjustments

Substack ↗

Against society's latest trend (did someone say padel?), one realisation: the relentless need to check the map and get back on your own path.

In a lovely analogy, Greg says a plane is off course 99% of the time. But it's a dynamically stable system: with small adjustments, it reaches its destination.

So, journal in hand, at the end of every week, on Sunday, Greg checks the actions he's taken, making sure they're in line with his relationships, his values.

What do you say, shall we readjust the course of the year from here?
