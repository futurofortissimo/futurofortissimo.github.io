🫘ff.92.1 Bazaar beans

Substack ↗

In this newsletter you won't find superfoods. But beans.

Dan Buettner is the expert when it comes to the regions with the most centenarians (Sardinia among them), the blue zones. His bestseller inspired the Netflix documentary on the subject.

93 years. The average age of the 9 siblings of the Melis family when they entered the Guinness World Records.

The secret?

The centenarian minestrone. Every day, at lunch, soup (recipe here): a trio of beans, carrots, onions, oregano, potatoes, celery, red peppers and a drizzle of oil (and pecorino) on top.

🦠ff.92.2 One more organ: the microbiome

Substack ↗

The microbiome (the layer of microbes lining the gut) is increasingly linked to mood, blood sugar and disease.

After all, if you think about it, the gut is where food spends most of its time in our body. So microbes control how fast molecules like sugars enter the bloodstream (and therefore the glycaemic response).

So much so that analysing (no pun intended) poop can win you the Olympics (Blummenfelt, world champion triathlete: 🇳🇴 ff.17.1 Norway is obsessive).

I Contain Multitudes by Pulitzer winner Ed Yong raises gut microbes to the level of our “own” cells. So much so that growing up without microbes is impossible (for example, they regulate blood pressure).

And the beans? Like other unprocessed foods, they're rich in fibre. And fibre fosters a healthy, diverse microbiome (and, as a result, less inflammation).

☕ff.92.3 Tea or coffee? Fuel for the microbiome

Substack ↗

The microbiome also has a sweet tooth for polyphenols.

Coffee and tea are packed with them. Another weapon against blood sugar spikes and diabetes?

La caffeina prende in prestito energia del futuro. Si lega ai ricettori dell’adenosina (molecola della stanchezza). Quando poi se ne va, però, tutta l’adenosina accumulata nel frattempo “ci colpisce ancora più fortemente”.Andrew Huberman,Using Caffeine to Optimize Mental & Physical Performance

Better not to go over 400mg of caffeine (caffeine guide at Starbucks). Balance it out with tea instead, which can reduce anxiety, thanks to L-theanine.
