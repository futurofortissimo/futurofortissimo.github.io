🚗ff.98.1 Driving simulator

Substack ↗

A driving simulator helps everyone: from Leclerc in F1 to your 50-year-old aunt who has finally signed up for her driving licence. But never as much as it helps Waymo. And autonomous driving.

Simulations are the closest thing we have to a time machine (without wheels). With ever shorter computing times, they replay thousands of possible (and impossible) scenarios.

So much so that Waymo's self-driving cars have virtually “driven” 10 billion miles, 1000x the ones they've driven in reality.

No wonder NVIDIA, born to optimise Battlefield's graphics, is now, as the simulated world has tangible effects on every technology, the most valuable company in the world.

🩼ff.98.2 Simulating Doctor House

Substack ↗

Using language models like ChatGPT, we can create “agents”: impersonations of business or social roles that, by interacting, create virtual societies, like in The Sims or an episode of Doctor House.

By simulating conversations between patients and doctors, between diseases and prognoses, a generic GPT “did an internship” and ended up becoming better (MedAgent-Zero) than models specifically trained on the entire scientific literature (Medprompt).

In the eternal dilemma of theory vs practice, experience seems to rule.

🦊ff.98.3 Sister moon, and the canticle of the nine-tailed creatures

Substack ↗

Laudato si', mi' Signore, per sora luna e le stelle: in celu l'ài formate clarite et pretiose et belle.San Francesco d’Assisi,Cantico delle creature

What a fortissimo mix. Let's talk about Sora and creatures. OpenAI's Sora, I mean. It creates videos from a text description. And it does so so realistically that industry experts suspect it learned the laws of physics “by watching videos”.

A nine-tailed fox? Even better: a flying fox! This and other fantastic beasts in Beyond Our Reality, an improbable National Geographic-style documentary created with Sora.

Speaking of bodies and simulations: 3 Body Problem, from the creators of Game of Thrones. Highly recommended!

Finally, for a philosophical trip on the subject, let me remind you of 🎼 ff.64 The metaphysics of the metaverse.

⚛️ff.98.4 Quantum systems

Substack ↗

Despite the potential of quantum computers, essential for simulating superconductors, drugs and nanotechnologies, the technology isn't mature yet (🎼 ff.69 Quantum?).

They make too many calculation errors, due to phenomena like quantum decoupling. But simulating quantum computers can help us.

But, but, but. Maybe studying qubits and Schrödinger's cats isn't necessary: Google DeepMind's AlphaFold3 (🎼 ff.4 Resurrected mammoths) has figured out quantum physics from experience.

From a text file listing the atoms present, the new version simulates the 3D structure and chemical interactions between DNA, RNA and small molecules (drugs). Before, it handled “only” proteins.
